# My team

> **Note:** Same design as [All](../All/all.md). **Type 1 — Sub-Navigation Rail.** Portal sub-nav remains above. Uses `GameplayScreen` shell from [global.md](../../../../global.md). Only the data filter differs.


## Difference from All

- Feed scoped to content related to the manager's own team: players from the team, coaching staff, official club account, competitions the team participates in, pundits commenting on related matches, and news about the team.
- All 6 account types (Pundit, Competition, Club, News, Player, Coaching Staff) appear; the filter is per-account, not per-type.
- Empty state: "No posts from your team yet."
