# Sideline Project Onboarding

## Product Overview

Sideline is a portrait-first football management simulation designed to deliver the depth of a full football manager experience in a pocket-sized mobile format. The product should let players run a football club from day to day, make meaningful sporting and operational decisions, and build a long-term club legacy without requiring a desktop-style interface or landscape orientation.

## Product Vision

The goal is to provide the breadth and strategic depth players expect from a complete football management game while making every workflow comfortable, clear, and efficient on a phone held in portrait mode. Sideline should not feel like a simplified companion app. It should feel like a complete management game whose information architecture, controls, and visual hierarchy were designed specifically for mobile.

## Player Role

The player acts as the club manager and is responsible for both short-term results and long-term development. Every major system should contribute to the central fantasy of making decisions, observing their consequences, adapting the club strategy, and progressing across seasons.

## Core Management Systems

Sideline should support squad selection, formation building, tactical instructions, player roles, lineup management, substitutions, and match preparation. It should include player transfers, contract decisions, negotiations, scouting, recruitment planning, and squad depth management. The academy should allow the player to discover, develop, promote, loan, or sell young talent as part of a sustainable long-term strategy. The broader management experience should also cover player development, fitness, morale, injuries, staff, competitions, schedules, club objectives, finances, and other interconnected systems expected from a full football management simulation as the product evolves.

## Portrait-First Experience

All product and interface decisions must prioritize portrait mobile use. Dense management information should be divided into focused screens, reusable cards, summaries, filters, tabs, sheets, and progressive detail instead of reproducing desktop dashboards at a smaller size. Important decisions must remain easy to reach, comparisons must remain readable, and complex workflows such as tactics or transfers must be usable with one hand where practical. The interface should reveal detail progressively without removing strategic depth.

## Product Principles

Sideline must preserve meaningful choice rather than adding complexity only for appearance. Systems should connect to one another so that tactics, recruitment, finances, development, morale, and results create understandable consequences over time. Repeated actions should be efficient, important outcomes should provide clear feedback, and the player should always understand the current club state, available options, and likely trade-offs. New features should strengthen the fantasy of managing and developing a football club while respecting the portrait-first, mobile-native direction.

## Scope Boundaries

Do not assume multiplayer, monetization, licensed leagues or clubs, a specific match simulation engine, or an online-only architecture unless those decisions are explicitly defined elsewhere in the project. Treat this document as the stable product direction and confirm unspecified game rules or business requirements before building behavior that depends on them.
